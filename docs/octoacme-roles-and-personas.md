# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Security / Compliance Lead

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Leads protect the team from avoidable risk by ensuring work meets security, privacy, and governance expectations before release and during ongoing operations.

### Responsibilities
- Review new features, integrations, and data flows for security and privacy risk
- Help define security requirements and acceptance checks for projects
- Coordinate with engineering on remediation plans for vulnerabilities or policy gaps
- Ensure compliance requirements are visible in planning, execution, and release processes
- Partner with PM and Product Lead to assess risk and decide when to escalate

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk across the project lifecycle
- Support safe delivery without slowing the team unnecessarily
- Make risk and controls visible to stakeholders early

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews and risk assessments
- Release readiness and compliance checkpoints
- Escalation updates with PM, Product Lead, and sponsor stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to review technical designs and secure-by-default implementation choices
- Supports Project Managers on risk tracking, escalation, and release readiness
- Partners with Product Managers to balance user needs with risk and compliance constraints
- Advises stakeholders on trade-offs and timing when issues affect launch readiness

---

## Data / Insights Lead

### Role Summary
The Data and Insights Lead helps the team understand whether the project is delivering value, identify adoption patterns, and make decisions based on evidence rather than assumptions.

### Responsibilities
- Define and monitor success metrics for the initiative
- Provide dashboards, reporting, and analysis for stakeholders
- Partner with product and business teams to interpret outcomes and trends
- Identify missing signals, instrumentation needs, and measurement gaps
- Support post-launch reviews and continuous improvement decisions

### Goals
- Improve decision quality through measurement and analysis
- Connect execution to business and customer outcomes
- Make impact visible across the project lifecycle

### Typical Communication
- Weekly or milestone health reviews
- Product and stakeholder reporting
- Retrospective analyses and decision support updates

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to validate whether priorities are achieving desired outcomes
- Supports Project Managers by surfacing delivery risks tied to adoption, usability, or performance
- Collaborates with Developers to define the right instrumentation and observability signals
- Helps stakeholders understand whether the project is improving customer value and operational effectiveness

---

## Delivery Lead / Engineering Manager

### Role Summary
The Delivery Lead or Engineering Manager ensures that technical work is planned, coordinated, and executed efficiently across the project while maintaining team health and delivery quality.

### Responsibilities
- Balance capacity and prioritize technical execution across workstreams
- Coordinate dependencies, technical risks, and sequencing with project leads
- Support team planning, delivery forecasting, and issue resolution
- Help maintain engineering quality, reliability, and sustainable pace
- Partner with PM and Product Lead to align execution with milestones and stakeholder expectations

### Goals
- Deliver predictable, high-quality engineering outcomes
- Reduce bottlenecks and improve team throughput
- Maintain a healthy, sustainable execution rhythm

### Typical Communication
- Sprint and milestone planning
- Dependency management and technical risk reviews
- Team health and delivery status updates

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Developers to align technical priorities and remove blockers
- Coordinates with Project Managers on timelines, dependencies, and delivery health
- Supports Product Managers by translating priorities into realistic implementation sequencing
- Escalates cross-team issues when engineering constraints affect roadmap commitments

---

## Customer Success / Enablement Partner

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Enablement partners connect project delivery to adoption, readiness, and stakeholder experience. They ensure that customers, support teams, and internal users can successfully use the solution after launch.

### Responsibilities
- Gather feedback from customers, support teams, and internal users
- Help validate readiness for launch, onboarding, and adoption
- Coordinate enablement materials, training, and communication support
- Identify product gaps that affect usability, adoption, or support burden
- Translate customer feedback into actionable inputs for product and delivery planning

### Goals
- Improve adoption and customer experience
- Reduce post-launch friction and support burden
- Ensure product changes are operationally ready for users

### Typical Communication
- User feedback loops and adoption reviews
- Stakeholder and support readiness updates
- Launch planning and post-release feedback sessions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Product Managers on customer problems, feedback, and launch readiness
- Collaborates with Project Managers on communication plans, stakeholder readiness, and milestone timing
- Works with Developers and QA to validate usability and operational readiness
- Feeds learning back into planning, backlog refinement, and continuous improvement

---

## QA / Release Manager

### Role Summary
QA and Release Managers ensure that work is ready to ship, validate quality in a structured way, and reduce risk during deployment.

### Responsibilities
- Define and oversee test strategy for features and critical workflows
- Coordinate release readiness checklists and validation steps
- Help manage go-live planning, rollback decision points, and deployment communication
- Monitor release quality and support post-deployment verification
- Identify release risks and escalate issues early

### Goals
- Improve confidence in what is being shipped
- Reduce defects and release instability
- Maintain a clear quality bar for every milestone and production change

### Typical Communication
- Test planning and release readiness reviews
- Deployment windows, smoke test results, and rollback decisions
- Status across QA, release planning, and production verification

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to validate functionality and defect closure
- Collaborates with Project Managers on release plans and milestone readiness
- Partners with Product Managers to confirm acceptance criteria are met
- Coordinates with Security / Compliance Lead when release risk or policy checks require review
